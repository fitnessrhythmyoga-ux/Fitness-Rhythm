# Fitness & Rhythm — Quiet Luxury

## Palette
- Background: `#F3F1EC` (ivory)
- Foreground: `#0D0D0D` (near-black)
- No accent color of any kind. Absolutely no blue, red, green, or gold.

## Typography
- **Display**: Bodoni Moda — high-contrast didone serif. Used for all major headings at expressive sizes.
- **Label / UI**: Jost — geometric sans, always widely letter-spaced, always in small-caps or uppercase.
- Never mix more than two families.
- Body copy stays minimal — rarely more than 30 words visible on a single viewport.

## Spacing & Layout
- Extreme negative space is the primary design material.
- Sections breathe. Padding is generous — never less than `py-24` on any section.
- No cards. No visible borders between content blocks. No dividers or rules.
- Full-bleed photography dominates. Text sits quietly within or beside imagery.
- Asymmetric editorial compositions preferred over equal columns.

## Imagery
- Full-bleed, cinematic, natural-light yoga and wellness photography.
- Desaturated or warmly toned images preferred.
- Images carry the emotional weight; type provides orientation only.

## Interactions
- All transitions: `ease-out duration-700` or slower. Never snappy.
- Scroll-triggered fade-up reveals on all major text blocks.
- Hover on images: subtle scale `1.03`, `duration-700`.
- Buttons: text + underline only. No fill color, no border-radius styling.
- Navbar: transparent → frosted glass (`backdrop-blur`) on scroll.

## Forbidden
- Colored buttons or colored text
- Drop shadows
- Cards or bordered containers
- Dense layouts with many elements per viewport
- Rounded corners on image containers
- Any color outside ivory and near-black
